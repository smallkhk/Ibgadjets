Coverage

Strong signal from the gate to the back flat.

One Starlink dish on the roof, wired down to a managed router and access points spread across the compound. Everybody gets their own speed lane — nobody can choke the line.

  • Your speed is yours.  Each account gets a dedicated lane, so one person streaming 4K doesn't slow your Zoom class.
  • One account, one device.  Hotspot sharing is blocked. Need more devices? Take a multi-device bundle.
  • Backup power.  Dish and router sit on inverter, so the net stays up when light goes.
  • Fair-use, plainly stated.  Unlimited means unlimited. We tell you upfront if a plan gets shaped after heavy use.

Questions

Before you buy.

Can I share my login with my roommate?

No. One account runs one device at a time. If you log in somewhere else, the first session drops. If two of you want to browse together, the multi-device bundles are cheaper than buying twice.

What happens when my data finishes?

Your session pauses and you land back on the login page. Buy a top-up and you're back instantly — you don't lose your account or your remaining days.

I don't live in the compound. Can I still buy?

Yes. The visitor bundles are built for that: bigger data, long validity, and you use it whenever you're around. Data doesn't expire until the validity date.

Do you take transfer?

OPay covers transfer, card and USSD. If you'd rather send USDT, we take BEP-20 on BSC and TRC-20 on TRON — paste the hash and it credits after confirmation.

Network is slow. What do I do?

Run the speed test in your dashboard first, then hit Support. It sends us your account, plan and current speed reading so we're not guessing.
