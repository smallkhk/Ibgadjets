How it works

Three steps, then forget about it.

The only part that needs a human is confirming your transfer. Everything after that is automatic.

STEP 01

Open an account

Your phone number is your username — no email, no forms to print. Residents add a flat number so we know where you are.

STEP 02

Transfer and upload

Pick a bundle, transfer to the account shown with your reference in the narration, then upload the receipt. We confirm it, usually within minutes.

STEP 03

Connect once

Join IB Gadgets, log in on the page that pops up, and that's the last time. Your phone is remembered — walk in tomorrow and it just connects.

Coverage

Built for one compound, done properly.

This isn't a reseller shuffling somebody else's bandwidth. It's one Starlink dish, one router, and a limit on how many people we sell to — because the whole point is that it stays fast.

  • Every flat in the compound — 2.4GHz reaches through the walls, 5GHz for anyone near the router.
  • The gate, the shop front and the compound yard — visitor bundles work anywhere in range.
  • Your speed is yours. Each bundle has its own cap, so one person downloading cannot flatten everybody else.
  • It keeps counting while NEPA is off — the dish and router run on backup, so your bundle isn't wasted.

Questions

The things people actually ask.

Do I have to log in every single day?

No. You log in once on each device. After that the router remembers your phone and connects it automatically, even after you leave and come back. You only see the login page again if you buy on a brand new device.

How many devices can I use?

Each bundle says how many. If you need more — a laptop, a smart TV, your children's tablets — ask us and we can raise the limit on your account without changing your plan.

What if my data finishes before the time?

The connection stops and you buy again. Nothing else changes — same account, same devices, same login. Unlimited bundles never run out on data, only on time.

Can I share my connection with my neighbour?

Your own devices, yes, up to your device limit. Turning your phone into a hotspot for other people is not allowed — the router can see when it happens, and accounts that keep doing it get suspended. If two of you want to share properly, buy a bundle with more devices; it's cheaper than two accounts.

What happens if the dish goes down?

Your bundle's data doesn't move while you can't browse, but the clock on time-limited plans keeps running. If we ever have a long outage, message us and we'll extend you.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer with a reference code, then upload the receipt — that's the main way. USDT on BSC or TRON also works if that's easier for you. Card payment is coming.
